# HMG-030 — Hotel Economics and Market Demand
## Authoritative Subsystem Specification v0.1

## 1. Purpose

This system converts regional travel demand, competition, hotel quality, reputation, room inventory, and player pricing into bookings and revenue. It also defines operating expenses, financing, financial reporting, and failure states.

---

## 2. Market Structure

Every scenario has a `Market` containing:

- base daily traveler volume,
- seasonal multipliers,
- weekday/weekend profile,
- special events,
- economic cycle modifier,
- segment shares,
- competitor hotels,
- travel-channel shares.

Demand is generated by segment and desired stay date.

---

## 4. Daily Potential Demand

For segment `s` and stay date `d`:

```text
PotentialDemand(s,d) =
 BaseDemand(s)
 * SeasonMultiplier(s,d)
 * DayOfWeekMultiplier(s,d)
 * EconomicMultiplier(d)
 * EventMultiplier(s,d)
 * ScenarioDemandMultiplier
```

Potential demand represents travelers entering the competitive set, not guaranteed bookings for the player.

---

## 5. Competitor Hotels

Each competitor has:

- room count,
- room categories,
- price by date/category,
- star class,
- reputation by category,
- amenity vector,
- location score,
- brand strength,
- availability.

Competitors may be simulated at aggregate level; their individual guests do not need map entities.

---

## 6. Hotel Choice Score

For each booking request, each eligible hotel receives:

```text
ChoiceScore =
 PriceUtility      * Wp +
 ReputationUtility * Wr +
 AmenityFit        * Wa +
 LocationUtility   * Wl +
 StarFit           * Ws +
 RoomFit           * Wroom +
 BrandUtility      * Wb
```

Segment weights sum to 1.

Then hotel-selection probability is computed with softmax:

```text
P(hotel i) = exp(ChoiceScore_i / T) / Σ exp(ChoiceScore_j / T)
```

`T` controls market randomness and is scenario-configurable.

---

## 7. Price Utility

Guest request has budget `B`. Hotel effective nightly price `P` includes mandatory fees known at booking.

```text
PriceRatio = P / B
```

Default utility:

- ratio <=0.75: 1.00
- 0.75–1.00: linearly 1.00 -> 0.80
- 1.00–1.25: 0.80 -> 0.25
- 1.25–1.50: 0.25 -> 0.00
- >1.50: ineligible

Segment price elasticity modifies the curve steepness.

---

## 8. Reputation Utility

Overall reputation is not sufficient. Booking segment evaluates weighted reputation categories.

Example Business:

- service 25%
- room 20%
- cleanliness 20%
- quiet 15%
- Wi-Fi/business 15%
- food 5%

Reputation utility is 0–1 from weighted category score /100.

Recent reviews receive greater influence through rolling decay.

---

## 9. Booking Lead Time

Booking requests are created ahead of arrival based on segment distribution.

Example defaults:

- airport/transit: median 2 days
- business: median 10 days
- leisure couple: median 21 days
- family: median 35 days
- conference/group: contract 60–365 days

Demand simulation must therefore maintain future-date inventory.

---

## 10. Reservation Inventory

For each date and room category:

```text
SellableInventory = PhysicalRooms
 - OutOfOrderRooms
 - Owner/ScenarioBlocks
```

Committed reservations consume inventory unless overbooking policy allows additional sales.

## 11. Booking Channels

Baseline channels:

| Channel | Commission default | Lead-time tendency |
|---|---:|---|
| Direct web | 0% | medium |
| OTA | 15% | short-medium |
| Corporate | negotiated | medium-long |
| Travel agent | 10% | medium |
| Group contract | negotiated | long |

## 12. Cancellation and No-Show

Each reservation stores:

- cancellation probability,
- no-show probability,
- cancellation deadline,
- cancellation penalty policy.

Cancellation is evaluated through deterministic scheduled events, not continuously.

Default booking behavior may vary with refundable/non-refundable rate plans.

---

## 13. Pricing Controls

Player may set price by:

- date range,
- day of week,
- room category,
- occupancy threshold rules,
- minimum/maximum price.

Dynamic rule example:

```text
IF projected_occupancy(date, category) >= 0.85
THEN rate_multiplier = 1.15
```

Rules execute in player-defined priority order at each revenue-management update.

---

## 14. Reference Market Price

Each room category gets a comparison-set median rate for a given date. The player’s `PricePosition` is:

```text
PricePosition = PlayerRate / ComparableMedianRate
```

This ratio feeds guest expectation and price-utility calculations.

---

## 15. Occupancy

```text
Occupancy = OccupiedRoomNights / AvailableRoomNights
```

For real-time daily display, denominator excludes `OutOfOrder` rooms but not unsold valid rooms.

---

## 16. ADR

```text
ADR = RoomRevenue / SoldRoomNights
```

Exclude taxes collected for government and non-room revenue.

---

## 17. RevPAR

```text
RevPAR = RoomRevenue / AvailableRoomNights
```

Also display equivalent:

`ADR * Occupancy`.

---

## 18. TRevPAR and GOP

Optional advanced metrics supported from v0.1 data model:

```text
TRevPAR = TotalOperatingRevenue / AvailableRoomNights
GOP = OperatingRevenue - DepartmentExpenses - UndistributedOperatingExpenses
```

Debt service, depreciation, tax, and capital expenditure are outside GOP.

## 21. Cash Accounting

All cash movements post to a transaction ledger:

```text
TransactionID
Timestamp
AccountCategory
Department
AmountMinorUnits
SourceEntityID|null
MemoCode
```

Nightly room revenue posts according to scenario accounting rule; default is at checkout for simplicity of cash flow, with accrued analytics tracked separately if needed.

---

## 22. Payroll

Employee base wage is hourly unless role is salaried.

Hourly payroll:

```text
BasePay = RegularHours * Wage
OvertimePay = OvertimeHours * Wage * OvertimeMultiplier
```

Default overtime multiplier: 1.5× after 8 scheduled work hours/day or jurisdiction override.

---

## 23. Loans

Loan object:

```text
Principal
AnnualInterestRate
TermMonths
PaymentFrequency
OriginationFee
CollateralRule
Covenants[]
```

Default amortizing monthly payment uses standard amortization formula.

The player sees total interest and next payment before accepting.

---

## 24. Overbooking

Player sets overbooking allowance by room category/date as either integer rooms or percentage.

`BookableInventory = SellableInventory + OverbookingAllowance`.

At arrival, if no assignable room exists, recovery priority is:

1. category-equivalent room,
2. free upgrade,
3. paid/negotiated upgrade if guest accepts,
4. room repair/clean acceleration where feasible,
5. relocate guest to competitor and compensate.

Failure produces a severe negative experience event.

---

## 25. Reputation Feedback to Demand

Reviews update reputation through exponentially weighted moving average.

Category reputation update:

```text
NewRep = OldRep * (1 - Alpha) + ReviewCategoryScore * Alpha
```

Default Alpha depends on review volume; target effective half-life is 90 review-equivalents (`BALANCE_TUNABLE`).

Low-volume hotels therefore move faster per individual review than large hotels.

---

## 26. Marketing

Marketing campaigns modify market funnel parameters for defined segments.

Campaign definition:

- cost/day or fixed cost,
- target segments,
- visibility multiplier,
- start/end,
- ramp-up,
- attribution decay.

Marketing increases the probability the hotel enters a request’s considered set. It does not guarantee selection.

---

## 27. Corporate and Group Contracts

A contract defines:

- dates,
- minimum/maximum room nights,
- negotiated room rate,
- event-space requirements,
- service-level requirements,
- cancellation terms,
- penalties,
- payment timing.

Player may accept only when physical and operational requirements are currently satisfiable or explicitly accept risk.

---

## 28. Financial Distress

Stages are based on cash and debt covenant conditions.

Baseline:

- Stage 0 Healthy: cash >= 30 days projected fixed expenses
- Stage 1 Tight: cash < 30 days
- Stage 2 Critical: cash < 7 days
- Stage 3 Insolvent: cash < 0 after available credit
- Stage 4 Default: scheduled debt/payroll obligation unpaid
- Stage 5 Bankruptcy/receivership after scenario-defined cure period

No stage transition is hidden from player.
